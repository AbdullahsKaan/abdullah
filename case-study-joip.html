The Challenge
01

Keeping a content-heavy site structured and consistent

JOIP.pk publishes a large volume of job and opportunity listings — postings from government departments, private organizations, educational institutions, and testing agencies. Each listing needs to communicate job title, location, qualifications, experience requirements, application details, and deadlines clearly and consistently.

The practical challenge on a site like this isn't any single article — it's maintaining consistent formatting and readability across a high volume of content, while applying on-page SEO practices to every piece published, without the structure breaking down over time.

My Approach
02

A repeatable content → SEO → publish workflow

Each piece of content followed the same process: prepare the job/opportunity information, structure it into readable sections (overview, vacancies, eligibility, qualification, experience, location, application procedure, and important dates), format and publish it in WordPress, then apply on-page SEO before it went live.

On-page SEO was handled through Yoast — reviewing SEO titles, meta descriptions, focus keyphrases, headings, and readability for each article, so that structure and search-friendliness were built in rather than added as an afterthought. Content was then maintained as part of the site's ongoing publishing operation.
