About Me

I'm Abdullah Khan, a WordPress Developer & SEO Specialist (On-Page SEO) based in Islamabad. I specialize in building, managing, and optimizing websites that not only look professional but also rank and convert.

My expertise combines WordPress development, SEO optimization, content creation, and digital support services into one complete workflow. From website creation to ranking and content growth, I handle everything with a results-driven approach.

I specialize in WordPress website development, Elementor/page builder design, WooCommerce setup, LMS platforms (LearnDash & Tutor LMS), website migration, cloning, backups, speed optimization, and performance improvements. Alongside development, I provide full on-page SEO services including keyword research, meta title & description optimization, XML sitemap setup, robots.txt configuration, Google Search Console and Google Analytics setup, and technical SEO fixes.

I also offer content writing and AI-assisted content services including blog writing, SEO blog writing, article writing, long-form and short-form content, website content writing, proofreading, editing, content humanization, and AI-based research using tools like ChatGPT and Claude AI.

In addition, I provide virtual assistant services such as customer support, chat handling, administrative support, content uploading, WordPress blog publishing, social media management (Facebook & LinkedIn), content scheduling, Canva assistance, and basic SEO tasks. My goal is simple: build fast, optimized, SEO-friendly websites and support businesses with content and digital operations that drive real growth.
