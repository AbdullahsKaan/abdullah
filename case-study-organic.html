Building an E-Commerce Site With a Custom Interactive Quiz

A look at building a WooCommerce shop for an Ayurvedic wellness brand, paired with a fully custom interactive quiz that scores user responses and delivers a personalized result — going beyond a standard store build.

The Challenge
01

More than just a shop

An Ayurvedic wellness brand wanted an e-commerce site that did more than sell products. Alongside a standard WooCommerce shop and blog, they wanted an interactive "dosha quiz" experience similar to what established brands in the category use to engage visitors and personalize recommendations.

That meant the project wasn't just a store build — it needed a genuinely interactive, scored quiz tool integrated into the site, plus a homepage design that matched the brand's natural, herbal positioning through several rounds of visual iteration.

My Approach
02

Shop first, then build the experience

The WooCommerce shop and blog structure were set up first to establish a solid e-commerce foundation. From there, the interactive quiz was built using the Quiz and Survey Master (QSM) plugin, configured so each answer contributes to a score that determines the personalized result shown at the end.

The homepage design went through several rounds of iteration — adjusting color, typography, and layout — to land on a look consistent with the brand's natural, herbal positioning rather than a generic WooCommerce theme feel.
