The Challenge
01

A full site build, on a launch deadline

A cryptocurrency project needed a complete website ahead of their token launch — a brand-heavy, visually distinct site that had to communicate credibility to their community, built from brand assets and reference material rather than an existing design.

Beyond the homepage build, the site needed a working chart and tokenomics display, image and content cleanup, and full responsiveness — all while the client iterated on exact visual details in the run-up to launch, with limited time to get it right.

My Approach
02

Build fast, refine in place

With a tight pre-launch timeline, the approach was to get a working structure — homepage, header, and footer — in place quickly using the client's brand assets, then refine specific sections like the chart and tokenomics display through focused rounds of feedback rather than trying to perfect every detail upfront.

Changes were staged in maintenance mode before going live, so the client's community only saw finished updates rather than work-in-progress states. Unused WooCommerce elements were also removed to keep the site lean and purpose-built for a project page rather than a store.

What I Did

From brand assets to a live launch site

01

Homepage & Brand Build

Built the homepage, header, and footer from the client's brand assets and reference material, establishing the visual identity for the project site.

02

Chart & Tokenomics Integration

Customized the chart and tokenomics display sections through several rounds of visual refinement to match the client's exact specifications.

03

Site Cleanup

Removed unused WooCommerce elements the client didn't need, keeping the site lean and purpose-built for a project launch page.

04

Image & Content Cleanup

Cleaned up and standardized site imagery, removing unused and unnecessary media across the site.

05

Responsive Design

Made the full site responsive across devices, checked carefully ahead of launch to make sure the experience held up on mobile.

06

Staging & Launch Support

Set up staging/maintenance mode so changes could be reviewed before the client's community saw them, and provided ongoing support through the site's public launch.
